About Me

I am a dedicated pharmacy professional and academic researcher with expertise in clinical pharmacy, hospital prescribing practices, patient safety, and advanced pharmacotherapy. My research aims to bridge the gap between clinical guidelines and everyday medical practice, with a particular focus on:

  • Clinical Pharmacy Practice: Evaluating pharmacist-led interventions to enhance clinical outcomes, including hospital prescribing practices and medication safety
  • Patient Safety: Investigating organizational factors that impact adverse drug events and medication error prevention in healthcare settings
  • Advanced Pharmacology: Exploring novel pharmacological approaches for pain management and pharmacotherapy optimization
  • Physician-Pharmacist Collaboration: Understanding best practices for integrating pharmacist recommendations into clinical decision-making
